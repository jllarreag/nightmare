# Product: Nightmare Resolver

Nightmare Resolver helps users face recurring nightmares. The user describes a nightmare; the app generates an
illustrated interactive story of 5 fixed steps with choices, optionally narrated by voice.

## Story arc (fixed, 5 steps)
1. Recreate: the nightmare, told gently and visually.
2. Take control: the user gains agency (grounding, breathing, noticing it is a dream).
3. Decision: the user picks between options that change the path, not the arc.
4. Rewrite: the ending is rewritten (Imagery Rehearsal Therapy style).
5. Closing: a learning and a short real-life coping tip.

## Clinical grounding
- Imagery Rehearsal Therapy (rewrite the ending, rehearse it).
- Graded exposure (start mild, never escalate beyond the user's own content).
- Cognitive reframing in the closing step.

## Non-goals
- No diagnosis, no medical advice, no claims of treatment.
- No user accounts. Sharing is opt-in and anonymized.

## Languages
Spanish and English. The story language follows the UI language.
